Release Notes
  1. Connect your Nano ESP32 via USB directly to your PC or Mac (not through a USB hub) and double click the Nano's RST button to enter recovery mode (a GREEN LED will strobe when successful).
  2. Click “Connect and Flash”, select “Nano ESP32 (bootloader)”, then press the Nano's RST button once when prompted.
  3. After installation, continue with the “Setup” steps listed on GitHub.
